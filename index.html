Five rules every magician follows

  1. Practice alone first. Do each move in a mirror until you do not have to think about it.
  2. Never say what is coming. If they know the ending, they watch for the trick.
  3. Do not repeat a trick for the same people. The second time they look for the secret.
  4. Look where you want them to look. People follow your eyes. That is called misdirection.
  5. Keep the secret. Teach it only to someone who wants to learn magic, not to people who just watched.
